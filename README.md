# The Impact of Volunteering on Youth Mental Health and Development

## 📊 AI-Powered Data Analysis Project

This project was completed as part of my **AI Data Analytics Internship with InAmigos Foundation**.

The project explores the relationship between **volunteering, youth mental health, social connection, and development** using research findings from relevant studies and reports.

## 🎯 Objective

The objective of this project is to examine available research and data to identify patterns and insights on how youth mental health and development are affected by volunteering.

## 🔍 Methodology

This project followed an AI-assisted research and data analysis workflow:

1. **Research** — Gemini and Perplexity were used to research relevant information and trace findings to their original sources.
2. **Dataset** — Evidence and findings were collected and organized into a research dataset.
3. **Analysis** — ChatGPT was used to help interpret the collected information and distinguish reported findings from observations.
4. **Organization** — Excel was used to organize and compare the findings.
5. **Report** — Microsoft Word was used to present the findings as a final report.

## 📈 Key Findings

* **28% lower odds of clinical-level emotional symptoms**
  One study found that young people who participated in volunteering by age 13 had approximately 28% lower odds of experiencing clinical-level emotional symptoms later in adolescence.

* **63% reported greater school connection**
  In a Student Volunteer Army study, 63% of students reported feeling more involved or connected with their school through volunteering.

These findings come from different studies, populations, and measures. Therefore, they should not be interpreted as proof that volunteering directly causes these outcomes.

## 💡 Key Insight

The collected findings indicate a positive association between volunteering and several aspects of youth well-being and development, particularly **mental well-being and social connection**.

Further research using comparable measures and populations could provide stronger evidence about these relationships.

## ⚠️ Limitations

* The studies were conducted in different countries.
* The studies involved different age groups and populations.
* Different aspects of mental health and development were measured.
* The findings mainly indicate associations rather than proving direct causation.
